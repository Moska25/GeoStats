A deterministic router over {{ approved|length }} approved metric functions. No language model, no API key, no network. Every answer shows the dataset, period, unit, vintage and formula it came from — and questions the published aggregates cannot support are refused with the reason.

The refusal is the feature. Geostat publishes a mean and a median. It does not publish a distribution, so no percentile can be reconstructed; it does not cross occupation with region, so that cell does not exist; it publishes gross pay, so take-home cannot be derived without a tax model. A system that answers those anyway is guessing politely. Four of the worked examples below are refusals, and they are the ones worth reading.
