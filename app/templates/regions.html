This is a cartogram, not a map. Every region gets one equal square regardless of its land area, so Racha-Lechkhumi is as visible as Kvemo Kartli and the picture is about people rather than hectares. The rows run roughly north to south and the columns roughly west to east. Shading is by rank rather than by value: Tbilisi is so far above the rest on most of these measures that a linear ramp would render eleven regions in one shade and the capital in another, which is a picture of the outlier, not of the country.
