What households report taking in and what they report spending, per household per month. These come from the Household Incomes and Expenditures Survey, which asks people. That makes them a different kind of number from the earnings series, which counts payroll, and the two are not interchangeable.

{% if lang != 'en' %}{% endif %}

The difference between these two lines is not a savings rate. Households under-report income more than they under-report spending in almost every survey of this kind, so the gap between reported income and reported expenditure is mostly a reporting artefact. Presenting it as dissaving, a savings rate, or a household deficit would be reading a survey design as an economic finding. It is shown below because hiding it would be worse, and it is labelled for what it is.
